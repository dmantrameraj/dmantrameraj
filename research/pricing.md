## 3. Verdict on the ₹999 hypothesis

**The plan contains a contradiction.** §3 names **freelancers** as the primary ICP; §30 hypothesises **₹999/month**. The market convention places freelancers at **₹299–499** and ₹999 at the **2–10 person team** tier. Both cannot be right.

**Recommendation (DECISION proposed, see [DECISIONS.md](../DECISIONS.md) D-004):** test three price points, not one, and let the ICP follow the price that converts:

| Plan | Price | Target | Includes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Free** | ₹0 | trial / fake-door | 1 upload, up to 50 contacts, 3 full opportunity reports (enough to prove value, not enough to finish the job) |
| **Solo** | **₹499/mo** | freelancers, consultants, coaches | 1 user, ≤300 contacts, monthly re-analysis, unlimited drafts |
| **Studio** | **₹1,499/mo** | 2–10 person agencies | 3 users, ≤2,000 contacts, weekly re-analysis, Sheets + Coretex sync |
| **Audit (one-off)** | **₹9,999–24,999** | the concierge offer (Phase 4) | done-for-you: cleaned data, ranked opportunities, 20 written messages, a call to walk through it |

Notes on the shape:
- **₹499 undercuts Zoho Standard (₹800)** — important, because a prospect's mental question is "cheaper or dearer than my CRM?", not "cheaper than folk?".
- **₹1,499 is inside the small-team band** and is where the real money is if the ICP narrows to agencies (recommended by [opportunity-gaps.md](opportunity-gaps.md) §3).
- **The one-off Audit is the most important row in this table.** [evidence-against.md](evidence-against.md) §4 shows every proven buyer in this category buys a *service*. Sell the audit first; it validates demand at 10–20× the SaaS price and funds the build.
- All prices exclude 18% GST — state that on the pricing page, since Zoho does and Indian buyers expect it.

**Do not set final prices until after interviews** (plan §30: *"Test pricing after problem validation"*).

## 4. Unit economics — AI cost per customer

**ESTIMATE, not measured.** Model prices from third-party aggregators; verify at the providers' own docs.

**Published rates (per 1M tokens, Sept 2026):**

| Model | Input | Output | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Claude Haiku 4.5 | $1 | $5 | [benchlm.ai](https://benchlm.ai/anthropic/api-pricing) |
| Claude Sonnet 5 | $2 | $10 | as above |
| Gemini 2.5 Flash | $0.30 | $2.50 | [geotoolbox.ai](https://geotoolbox.ai/blog/gemini-api-pricing) |
| Gemini 3.8 Flash | $0.75 | $3.75 | [aimagicx.com](https://www.aimagicx.com/blog/llm-api-pricing-comparison-2026) |

Both providers offer a **50% Batch discount**, and prompt caching further reduces repeated-context cost.

**Assumed workload per analysis run:** 500 contacts × (~800 input tokens of contact context + ~300 output tokens of reason/action/draft) = **400k input / 150k output**.

| Model | Standard | With 50% Batch |
|---|---|---|
| Claude Haiku 4.5 | $0.40 + $0.75 = **$1.15** (≈₹101) | ≈$0.58 (≈₹51) |
| Claude Sonnet 5 | $0.80 + $1.50 = **$2.30** (≈₹202) | ≈$1.15 (≈₹101) |
| Gemini 2.5 Flash | $0.12 + $0.375 = **$0.50** (≈₹44) | ≈$0.25 (≈₹22) |

**Conclusion (ESTIMATE):** a 500-contact monthly re-analysis costs roughly **₹22–₹200** depending on model and batching. Against a ₹499 plan that is **4–40% of revenue** — workable, but *not* negligible at the low tier with a cheap-model-only design.

**Design implications:**
1. **Two-stage pipeline.** Cheap model (Gemini Flash / Haiku) for normalisation, entity resolution, dormancy classification and scoring across all contacts; a stronger model **only** for the top ~10–20 opportunities that get a reason + a drafted message. This is the difference between ₹200 and ₹30 per run.
2. **Use the Batch API** for the nightly/weekly sweep — the 50% discount is free money for work nobody is waiting on.
3. **Cache the system prompt and the user's business profile** (services offered, tone, pricing) — it repeats on every contact.
4. **Re-analyse on change, not on schedule.** Re-scoring an unchanged contact every month is pure cost.
5. **Cap contacts per plan** (300 / 2,000 above) so the cost line cannot run away. Attio's **seats + usage credits** model is the honest structural precedent if usage varies wildly ([coffee.ai](https://www.coffee.ai/articles/attio-crm-pricing-2026)).
6. **Never route the whole database to the model** — plan §25 already says this, and it is also the main cost lever.

### Fixed costs that actually threaten the margin

| Item | Annual | = customers at ₹499/mo (₹5,988/yr) |
|---|---|---|
| Gmail CASA audit (~$1,500 mid-estimate ≈ ₹1.32L), **recurring yearly** | ₹1.32L | **~22 customers, every year, for one integration** |
| Hosting/DB (Hostinger VPS class) | ~₹12–24k | 2–4 customers |
| AI (100 customers × 500 contacts, batched cheap model) | ~₹26k | ~5 customers |

**The dominant cost in this business is not AI — it is Gmail's compliance audit.** That single line is the strongest argument for the integration re-ordering recommended in [opportunity-gaps.md](opportunity-gaps.md) §3: **Sheets/CSV → Coretex → Calendar → Gmail (last, and only past ~100 paying customers).**
